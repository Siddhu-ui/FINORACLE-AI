## National Pension System (NPS) Structure
The National Pension System (NPS), regulated by PFRDA, is a voluntary, defined-contribution retirement savings scheme open to Indian citizens. NPS offers two account types: Tier I (the primary retirement account with lock-in until age 60 and tax benefits under Section 80CCD(1), additional ₹50,000 deduction under Section 80CCD(1B) in the Old Tax Regime, and employer contribution benefits under Section 80CCD(2) available under both regimes) and Tier II (a voluntary flexible savings account with no withdrawal lock-in).

## Asset Allocation and Lifecycle Funds
NPS subscribers can choose between Active Choice (allocating across Equity Class E up to 75%, Corporate Debt Class C, Government Securities Class G, and Alternative Assets Class A) or Auto Choice Lifecycle Funds (Aggressive, Moderate, or Conservative), which automatically rebalance from higher equity exposure in younger years toward safer debt instruments as the subscriber approaches retirement. Low fund management charges and long-term compounding make disciplined retirement contributions vital for beating post-retirement inflation.
